    From the developer

    Why I Built This Demo

    Most bar websites die the same way. The site goes up, the events page says whatever it said at launch, and nobody touches it again because touching it means emailing a developer. So regulars stop checking, and the one place that always knows what's on tonight is a chalkboard.

    This page is built the other way around. The weekly lineup is data, and the site works out tonight, this week, and next month on its own. Old shows drop off by themselves. And when a new band gets booked, the owner adds it right on the site, in under a minute. The OWNER DEMO link below the calendar is live if you want to try that yourself.

    The Brass Pelican is not a real bar; the calendar is the point.
