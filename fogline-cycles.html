§ 04 · For the curious

Why I built this demo

Most repair shops take bookings on a phone nobody can reach, because both hands are inside a bottom bracket. Jobs live in voicemail, text threads, and sticky notes on the bench. Then a callback gets missed and a customer walks.

This page is the alternative. The customer picks a slot, describes the bike and the problem before showing up, and says up front how much extra work they will approve. The shop gets a board it can run the day from. Fewer calls, fewer surprises, nothing on a sticky note.

Customer Select service→ Select time→ Bike intake→ Confirm→ Work tag
↓ same data, no phone call
Shop Appointment appears→ View intake→ Service bike→ Mark complete

One HTML file, no backend, so everything stays in your browser. In a real deployment the bookings would land in a database, text the shop, and sync a calendar. That part is plumbing. The flow you just used is the hard part.
